# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to building SignPakCommons as a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative project. We welcome contributors from all backgrounds, skill levels, and perspectives, and we expect all participation in this project to be constructive and professional.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being kind, respectful, and patient with others
- giving and receiving feedback constructively
- focusing on the project goals, user impact, and technical quality
- acknowledging the work and effort of other contributors
- helping others learn and grow in a supportive way

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory, insulting, or exclusionary language
- threatening or abusive communication
- repeatedly derailing discussions or blocking progress without cause
- sharing private information without consent
- behavior that undermines the safety or dignity of contributors or users

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- repository communication channels
- meetings, events, and project-related collaboration
- any public or private activity associated with SignPakCommons

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

- editing or removing harmful content
- asking for a change in behavior
- temporarily restricting participation
- removing access or banning contributors in serious or repeated cases

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository owner contact channels or the appropriate project communication route. Include as much context as possible, such as links, screenshots, dates, and the people involved.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially, with a focus on maintaining a safe and respectful community.
